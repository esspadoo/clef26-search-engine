\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
All experiments were conducted on our machines, utilizing a computer with an AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, 32 GB RAM and a NVIDIA RTX 3090 GPU (10496 CUDA Cores, boost clock 1.70 GHz, 24 GB of GDDR6X memory, 35.58 TFlops) for resource-intensive workload and our laptops for standard workloads and refining of the programs. We have four laptops, the two most powerful are a MacBook Air M3 and one with no dedicated GPU, 16GB RAM and an Intel core i7-13700H.
For reproducibility, all the environment settings have been documented and are freely available at the Bitbucket's repository https://bitbucket.org/upd-dei-stud-prj/seupd2526-retrix/src/master/ or easily obtained by following the instructions in this paper.  The evaluation metric for the task is only MRR@5 but in our runs we also report Recall@100 to provide a more complete picture of the differences between configurations as well as the results obtained.

\subsection{Dataset}
\label{sec:dataset}

The dataset consisted of 10,000 scientific publication that had to be paired with tweets-like posts written in three languages: German, French and English. There aren't explicit references such as URLs in any query revised but any tweet contains implicit references to a scientific paper such as mention of the authors or key-words related to the paper. The datasets files are all in JSON format. The publications' dataset contains the columns pubkey (unique identifier), title, abstract, venue / location and authors while the queries' datasets contain the columns index, pubkey (ID of the positive scientific paper that has to be matched with the query) and text.
